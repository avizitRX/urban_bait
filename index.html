Our story

Who we are – UK carp bait specialists

Whether you are looking for a rigorously tested bait range that has stood the test of time over multiple decades, or you are experimenting with making your own mixes, here you will find some of the finest ingredients and nutritionally balanced baits, boilies, additives and pellets available in the UK.

Having started my angling journey back in 1981 I have personally field tested every blend at every step across all seasons. In the early days there was no one manufacturing or selling carp bait and I was one of a small group of likeminded carp anglers who was involved in pioneering readymade boilies, additives and rigs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right boilie?

Start with the range that suits your swim and season – browse the Nutcracker, Strawberry, Red Spicy Fish, Tuna & Garlic, Pink Himalayan Salt and Fully Loaded ranges, then add a matching hookbait. Not sure? Contact us and we’ll help.

Can I make my own bait mix?

Yes. Our additives, oils and particles are made for anglers who like to experiment with their own potent mixes.

What are hookbaits sold in?

Hookbaits are usually sold in jars of 100 grams.
